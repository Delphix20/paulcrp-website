01 / ABOUT

A little clarity, from the start.

This Privacy Policy explains how Hello, Beautiful! (the "app") handles information when you use its photo-editing features, save edits, make an in-app purchase or visit this policy page. "We", "us" and "our" refer to the provider of Hello, Beautiful!, available through paulcrp.com.

The app is a local photo editor, not a social network or a cloud photo service. You do not need to create an account with us. We do not operate a server that receives your photos or facial analysis for editing. Apple services, websites you open and destinations you choose when sharing can involve separate online processing, explained below.

This policy describes the current app. It does not replace Apple's privacy notices or the notices of other services you choose to use. It is a transparency notice, not a waiver of your statutory rights or a request for blanket consent.

02 / PHOTOS & PERMISSIONS

You choose what to open.

The app processes the photos you select or capture, their image dimensions and orientation, and the adjustments you make. This information is needed to display, analyze, edit and export the image. Your original photo is not overwritten.

Open a photo
Apple's system photo picker lets you select individual photos without giving the app access to your entire library. If a selected item is stored in iCloud, Apple's system may download it to your device before supplying it to the app.
Camera
Camera permission is requested when you choose to take a photo. Camera images used by the editor are processed on your device. The app does not run a remote camera stream or record audio.
Save to Photos
The app requests permission to add an edited copy to your photo library. This does not require the editor to browse unrelated photos.
Other permissions
The app does not request your contacts, microphone, precise location or advertising tracking permission for its editing features.

You can change camera and photo permissions in iOS or iPadOS Settings. Refusing a permission prevents the related action, such as taking or saving a photo; it does not give us permission to access that information another way. You can use available editing functions without enabling the camera.

03 / FACE ANALYSIS & AI

For your edit. Not your identity.

To place effects accurately, the app uses bundled machine-learning models and device frameworks to locate faces, facial landmarks and regions such as skin, hair, eyes, eyebrows, lips and visible teeth. It may calculate face geometry, landmark coordinates, confidence values and pixel-level selection masks, including masks for visible neck or body skin and the background.

All of this analysis runs on your device. It is used to position and blend the effects you choose, render previews, refine selections and produce your edited photo. Embedded components, including MediaPipe and Core ML-based processing, do not require sending your portrait to a model provider or an external AI service.

  • We do not upload, receive, sell or share your photos, face landmarks or facial analysis for these editing operations.
  • We do not use them to identify you, match your identity across photos, authenticate you, build an identity database, or access Face ID enrollment data.
  • We do not use your photos or face analysis to train, improve or fine-tune machine-learning models, or supply them to others for that purpose.
  • The app does not infer your health, ethnicity, beliefs or emotional state, or make decisions about you with legal or similarly significant effects.

Automated landmarks and generated analysis masks are working data held locally for the editing session, not a persistent face-identification profile. They are released when the editing session is cleared or the app process ends and can be recalculated when a saved draft is reopened. A saved draft can retain your photo, adjustment values and manual brush or selection instructions, as described next.

A face photo and its geometry can be personal or sensitive information under applicable law even when not used for identification. We treat this information as private editing data; we do not claim that local processing makes privacy protections unnecessary.

04 / LOCAL STORAGE

Your edit stays with you.

Last edit
When "Remember last edit" is enabled, the app stores one working photo and its editing instructions inside its local app storage so you can continue later. A new saved draft replaces the previous one. Turning this setting off deletes the saved draft; you can also use "Delete last edit" in Settings.
Saved looks
Saved looks contain the name you choose, effect strengths, colors and related preset settings. They do not contain the portrait or an identity template. You can remove saved looks individually.
Preferences
The device stores preferences such as haptics, camera choice, selfie mirroring, tips and export size, along with state needed for the app's interface.
Working files
Rendering uses local memory and temporary files. An export can create a temporary image file while the save or share action is being completed.

The app marks its draft and saved-look storage directory as excluded from standard device cloud backup. It does not provide its own cloud synchronization for those files. Other operating-system data, preferences, device transfers or backups may be handled according to Apple's services and your settings. This exclusion does not apply to copies you export to Photos, Files or another service.

05 / EXPORTS & SHARING

Nothing is posted for you.

Export creates a new edited image. The current JPEG export is encoded from rendered pixels and does not copy the original photo's GPS location or original EXIF metadata into the exported file. Technical information necessary for the image, such as its dimensions, orientation and color information, may still be present. The image itself can reveal a person's identity, surroundings or other private details.

Saving to Photos places the copy in your library. If you use iCloud Photos or another backup service, that service may subsequently upload the saved copy under your settings. Sharing uses Apple's share interface; the app or destination you choose receives the exported image and applies its own privacy practices. We do not automatically publish your edits or receive a copy.

Deleting a draft or uninstalling the app does not delete images already saved to your library, sent to someone else, stored in a backup or posted on another service. Manage those copies at their respective destinations.

06 / PURCHASES

Payments are handled by Apple.

Subscriptions and purchase restoration use Apple's App Store and StoreKit. Apple processes your Apple Account and payment information. We do not receive your payment-card number, billing address or Apple Account password through the app.

StoreKit provides the app with information needed to verify and manage access, including product and transaction identifiers, subscription validity and expiration, revocation or refund status, renewal or billing-grace status, and introductory-offer eligibility. Apple can also provide the original app-acquisition date, which the app can use to preserve eligible early-user or previously available access. Verification and access decisions take place in the app using Apple's signed information; we do not run our own purchase-validation server.

Loading subscription options, purchasing, checking eligibility and restoring purchases can connect to Apple. Apple may make sales, financial, subscription and aggregated usage reports available to us through its developer services. These reports are not a feed of your photos or facial analysis.

Apple's Privacy Policy governs Apple's handling of this information. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account subscription settings. Removing the app or its local data does not cancel a subscription. Pricing, renewal and any eligible trial are disclosed in the purchase flow; this policy does not alter those terms.

07 / DIAGNOSTICS & INQUIRIES

Only what is needed to help.

The app does not embed advertising SDKs, behavioral analytics SDKs or a third-party crash-reporting service. It does not send us a history of the tools you use, the slider values you choose or the portraits you edit.

Apple may collect and share app diagnostics and aggregate statistics according to its platform settings and policies. Where available to us, diagnostics can include app and operating-system versions, device type, crash information and technical performance details. The app can also write technical errors to local system logs. These mechanisms are not used to intentionally attach photos, facial landmarks or editing masks to a report.

If you voluntarily contact us through the contact options on paulcrp.com, we receive the information you send, such as your message, reply details, app version and any attachments you choose to include. We use it to answer your inquiry, investigate an issue, handle privacy requests and maintain necessary correspondence. Opening the website does not automatically attach your photo or editing session.

Please avoid sending sensitive photos, financial details or information about other people unless needed for your request and you are entitled to share it. Voluntarily sending an image for support is separate from the app's ordinary on-device editing.

08 / THIS WEBSITE

A page, not a photo upload.

Reading this policy makes an ordinary web request. The site is hosted through GitHub Pages and delivered and protected by Cloudflare. Hosting and network providers can process IP addresses, requested URLs, request times, browser or device headers, response status and security or network-error information to deliver pages, prevent abuse and maintain reliability.

This policy page has no advertising or analytics scripts, no photo-upload form, and no embedded social-media widgets. Its font and image assets are hosted on the same website rather than requested from a third-party font service. The page itself does not set advertising cookies, create browser-storage identifiers or perform browser fingerprinting. Hosting or security infrastructure may use operational mechanisms necessary to deliver and protect the site.

Other pages on paulcrp.com, including the main app catalog, may use website analytics such as Google Analytics. Following a link to another page is separate from using the app or reading this policy. Your browser and service settings affect that processing.

Further information is available in the GitHub Privacy Statement, Cloudflare Privacy Policy and, for pages that use its services, Google Privacy Policy. These providers do not receive your app portraits merely because you read this page.

09 / RECIPIENTS

No sale. No advertising profiles.

We do not sell or rent personal information, share it for cross-context behavioral advertising, or use portrait and face information for marketing. The app does not access the advertising identifier or track your activity across other companies' apps and websites.

Information can nevertheless reach others in these limited circumstances:

  • Your choices: a service, app or person you select when sharing, saving to a cloud-enabled library or sending an inquiry.
  • Platform and service providers: Apple for App Store services and applicable diagnostics; website hosting, security and communication providers for web requests or messages. They receive information relevant to their function, not an automatic copy of your local photo library.
  • Legal and safety needs: information actually held by us may be disclosed where legally required, to respond to valid legal process, protect rights or address fraud or security incidents, subject to applicable safeguards.
  • Business changes: if operation of the app is transferred, information actually held by us may be transferred where lawful and with appropriate protections and notice. Such a transfer does not give us access to photos or analysis stored only on your device.

There is no account-based cloud gallery, public feed, data-broker integration or remote facial-recognition service in the app.

10 / PURPOSES & LEGAL BASES

Specific uses, not open-ended access.

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis for processing for which we are responsible, the applicable basis depends on the activity:

  • Providing requested services: processing necessary to provide the editing, saving and access-management functions you request, or respond to a request connected with those services.
  • Consent: where consent is legally required for an optional activity, we rely on that consent and respect its withdrawal. A system permission controls access to a device feature; it is not blanket consent to unrelated processing.
  • Legitimate interests: necessary and proportionate handling of technical information and correspondence to maintain a secure website, diagnose faults and respond to inquiries, balanced against your rights and expectations.
  • Legal obligations: information that must be retained or processed to meet applicable legal, accounting or valid regulatory requirements.

We do not rely on these grounds to upload local portraits, perform advertising profiling or introduce an undisclosed use of face information. We do not require you to provide a portrait by law; providing one is necessary only if you want to edit it. Without a relevant permission or required purchase verification, the corresponding feature may not be available.

11 / RETENTION & DELETION

You can leave less behind.

  • Session analysis: kept in working memory while needed for the active editing session; released when that session is cleared or the process ends. It is not saved as a permanent recognition record.
  • Last-edit photo and instructions: remain in local storage until replaced by a new saved draft, deleted through Settings, removed by disabling "Remember last edit", or removed with the app's data.
  • Saved looks: remain locally until you delete them or remove the app's data. They are separate from the last-edit draft.
  • Preferences: remain until changed, reset or removed with app data, subject to operating-system backup and restoration behavior.
  • Temporary exports: are used for save and share operations and may remain temporarily if an operation is interrupted, until app cleanup or operating-system removal.
  • Exported copies: remain wherever you saved or shared them until removed there. Photos and backup services may have their own recently-deleted folders and retention periods.
  • Inquiries and records we receive: retained only as needed to resolve the matter, maintain a necessary support history, meet legal obligations or establish, exercise or defend legal claims. The relevant criteria are the purpose, sensitivity, resolution of the inquiry and applicable retention duties; information no longer needed should be deleted or anonymized.
  • Provider records: Apple purchase records, hosting/security logs and platform diagnostics follow the relevant provider's retention controls and legal obligations. We cannot erase Apple's transaction history from your device.

To remove local editing data, use the draft and saved-look controls in app Settings and close the active editor. Deleting the app normally removes its app-container data; offloading an app is different and can preserve that data. Separately review exported photos, device backups and shared copies. We cannot remotely retrieve or delete information held only on your device.

12 / YOUR RIGHTS

Your choices matter.

Depending on where you live and the law that applies, you may have rights to know whether personal information is processed; access or obtain a copy; correct inaccuracies; request deletion; restrict processing; receive portable information; withdraw consent; and complain to a data-protection authority. Exceptions can apply, for example where records must be retained by law.

Your right to object: where processing relies on legitimate interests, you may object on grounds relating to your situation. You may also object to direct marketing. We do not use app photos or face analysis for direct marketing.

Where applicable, regional privacy laws may also give you rights to opt out of sale, sharing or targeted advertising, limit certain uses of sensitive personal information, use an authorized agent, or appeal a denied request. We do not sell or share app personal information for advertising and do not carry out the advertising activities described above, so there is no such app activity to opt out of. Exercising rights will not result in unlawful discrimination.

For a request concerning information we actually hold, use the contact options at paulcrp.com and identify Hello, Beautiful! and your request. We may ask for proportionate information to verify the request or an agent's authority, but will not require you to send a face photo merely to make a privacy request. We will respond within the period required by applicable law and explain any lawful limitation or refusal.

For local-only drafts and looks, the app's controls are the direct way to access or delete that information: we have no remote copy to search. For Apple's information, use Apple's privacy controls and Data and Privacy portal. You can withdraw device permissions in Settings at any time; withdrawal does not make prior lawful processing unlawful. Where consent is the legal basis, you may withdraw it through the relevant setting or by contacting us.

You may raise concerns with your competent supervisory authority without first contacting us. For example, relevant authorities include the European data-protection authorities and the Information Commissioner's Office, where their laws apply.

13 / CHILDREN & OTHER PEOPLE

Respect every person in the picture.

The app is not designed to solicit personal information from children under 13 or below a higher minimum age applicable to their use of the service. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through support or website interactions in violation of applicable law. If you believe such information has been provided to us, contact us through paulcrp.com so we can assess and, where required, delete it.

Parents and guardians should supervise children's device permissions, purchases and sharing. An App Store content age rating is not the same as a legal age for consenting to personal-data processing.

Only edit or share images that you are entitled to use, and obtain permission where required from the people depicted or their guardians. Do not use edits to impersonate, harass or mislead others. The app's cosmetic effects are image modifications, not medical advice or assessments of a person's health or identity.

14 / SECURITY & TRANSFERS

Less data leaving your device.

Local processing reduces the need to transmit portraits. The app uses the operating system's app sandbox and storage protections, and draft photo files use iOS file-protection options. Website connections use HTTPS. These measures reduce risk but no device, storage system or internet connection can be guaranteed completely secure.

Use a device passcode, keep the operating system updated and take care with backups, shared devices and exported photos. Anyone with access to your unlocked device or exported files may be able to see your images.

Ordinary editing does not transfer portraits or facial analysis internationally because it runs on your device. Apple services, website infrastructure and voluntary communications may involve processing in locations outside your place of residence, with different privacy laws. The providers' notices explain their processing locations and transfer arrangements. Where we are responsible for a restricted transfer, applicable law requires an appropriate legal mechanism, such as an adequacy decision or approved contractual safeguards. You can request information about applicable safeguards through paulcrp.com.
