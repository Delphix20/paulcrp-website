MatchFlare

Privacy Policy and Terms of Use

Last updated: October 10, 2026 · applies to version 2.0 and later

MatchFlare is a football app for live scores, fixtures, tables, line-ups, statistics, news, alerts, widgets and Live Activities. Before version 2.0 it was published as “World Football Cup 2026”. This page explains what information the app uses, what it does not use, and the terms that apply when you use it.

The short version

  • You use MatchFlare without an account. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or password.
  • There are no ads, no analytics software and no tracking in the app. We do not sell or share your data for advertising.
  • To send you the alerts you ask for, our server keeps a random ID for your installation, the notification token Apple gives the app, and the teams, players and matches you follow.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never see your card details.
  • You can erase what our server holds at any time in the app: Settings › Delete my data.

1Who we are

MatchFlare (the “app”) is made and published by PAUL CRP - App Development Studio, Romania (“we”, “us”). We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy.

Questions or requests about privacy: devpaulcrp@gmail.com

2What stays on your device

The app keeps the following in its own storage on your iPhone or iPad:

  • the teams, players and matches you follow, the competitions you pin and the alerts you switch on
  • your settings, such as news languages, light or dark theme and the app icon you chose
  • whether you have MatchFlare Premium
  • football information the app has already downloaded (scores, tables, crests, news), so that screens and widgets open quickly
  • a random installation ID and a secret key, created by the app and kept in the device’s keychain

Widgets and Live Activities read this information on the device. If you delete the app, its storage is deleted with it. The installation ID in the keychain may remain on the device and be used again if you install the app again. Apple may include the app’s storage in the device backups you have switched on.

3What our server receives and keeps

Alerts have to be sent from a server, and the server has to know where to send them and what you want to hear about. For that purpose the app sends us the following. None of it contains your name, email address, phone number or Apple Account, and we do not use it to work out who you are.

4Information that goes to others

  • Apple. Notifications and Live Activity updates are delivered by Apple’s notification service. Purchases, refunds and restoring purchases are handled by the App Store. If you have agreed to share diagnostics with app developers, Apple may send us crash and performance reports that do not identify you. Apple processes this under the Apple Privacy Policy.
  • Our hosting provider. Our database is kept by a cloud hosting provider in the European Union (Ireland). The provider processes the data in section 3 on our behalf and under our instructions. A request from the app may pass through the provider’s servers in other countries on its way there.
  • Image servers. Club and competition crests and news photos are loaded by the app directly from the servers that host them. As with any image on the internet, those servers receive your IP address and basic technical details of the request.
  • News publishers. Headlines, short summaries and photos come from news publishers and are shown with their source. When you open a story, the publisher’s own web page is displayed inside the app. That page belongs to the publisher, may use cookies, and is covered by the publisher’s own privacy policy, not this one.
  • Authorities. We disclose information if the law requires it or if it is needed to protect our rights, the service or other people.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with anyone for their advertising or marketing.

5What the app does not do

  • It shows no advertising and contains no advertising or analytics software.
  • It does not track you across other companies’ apps and websites, and it does not use the advertising identifier.
  • It does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • It has no accounts, no chat and no way of posting content.
  • It makes no automated decisions about you and builds no profile of you.

6Why we may use this information

Where the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or similar law applies, we rely on these legal grounds:

  • Providing the service you ask for (performance of a contract): keeping your follows and alert choices and delivering alerts, Live Activities and paid features.
  • Your consent: notifications are sent only if you allow them when iOS asks. You can withdraw that permission at any time in the Settings app.
  • Our legitimate interests: keeping the service secure, reliable and free from abuse, and verifying purchases. We keep this to the minimum described in section 3.
  • Legal obligations, where they apply.

7How long information is kept

  • Your installation’s registration (section 3) is kept while you use the app. It is erased as soon as you choose Settings › Delete my data, and automatically when the app has not been opened for 12 months.
  • A notification token is removed when Apple tells us it is no longer valid, for example after the app has been deleted.
  • Live Activity tokens are removed about two days after they were last used.
  • Stored player search results are removed within about a week.
  • Technical logs are kept by the hosting provider for a short period, as needed for security and troubleshooting.
  • Apple keeps purchase records according to its own policies.

8Your choices and rights

  • Notifications: allow or stop them in the Settings app, and choose which alerts you get under Settings inside MatchFlare.
  • Live Activities: allow or stop them for MatchFlare in the Settings app.
  • Follows: add or remove teams, players and matches at any time.
  • Erasure: Settings › Delete my data removes your follows and alert choices from the device and erases your installation’s registration from our server. Your purchase is not affected.

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for access to your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its use, to receive it in a portable form, and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no name or email address for you, the in-app button is the quickest way to erase your data. For any other request, write to devpaulcrp@gmail.com and we will tell you how we can find the record of your installation. We do not treat anyone differently for using these rights.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Romania this is the National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing (ANSPDCP, dataprotection.ro); you may also contact the authority of the country where you live.

California and other US states: we do not sell personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

9Where information is processed

Our database is in the European Union. Our hosting provider, Apple and the servers that host images and news pages may process the limited data described in section 4 in other countries, including the United States, under the safeguards described in their own policies, such as standard contractual clauses.

10Security

Data travels between the app and our server over encrypted connections. The secret key that protects your installation’s settings is stored on the server only in hashed form, and purchases are checked against Apple’s digital signature. No method of sending or storing data is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

11Children

MatchFlare is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13, or under the age at which your country lets a person agree to the use of their data. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, write to us and we will delete it.
