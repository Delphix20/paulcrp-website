Privacy Policy for Cyphre: Puzzle Ring Challenge

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Cyphre: Puzzle Ring Challenge ("Cyphre", the "App") is a puzzle game for iPhone and iPad developed by Paul Crapatureanu ("we", "us", "our"), based in Romania. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App uses, how it is used, who else receives it, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

The Short Version

  • There are no accounts. The App never asks for your name, email address, phone number or contacts.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored on your device. We do not receive them and we do not run servers that collect your data.
  • Unless you buy Premium, the App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect device and usage information to serve and measure those ads, subject to the choices described below.
  • Purchases and Game Center are handled by Apple.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

To let you continue where you left off, the App saves the following on your device only:

  • Game progress: completed levels, stars, best move counts, hints, daily puzzle streak and the tutorials you have seen.
  • Settings and choices: sound, music, haptics, color-blind symbols, reduced motion and the theme you selected.
  • Purchase status: whether Premium has been unlocked.
  • Ad counters: how many full-screen ads have been shown, which the App uses to space out its occasional offer to remove ads.

This information stays in the App's storage on your device and is not sent to us. Depending on your Apple settings, it may be included in backups of your device. You can erase it at any time by using Settings > Reset all progress in the App or by deleting the App.

2. Information Collected by Third Parties

2.1 Google AdMob (advertising)

The free version of the App shows ads through Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC and its affiliates ("Google"). Ads appear as a banner on the level list, as full-screen ads between levels, and as optional rewarded videos that you can watch to earn hints or to continue a level.

To deliver, measure and protect these ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Your IP address and the approximate location that can be derived from it (not your precise location)
  • Device and software information, such as device model, operating system version and language
  • The advertising identifier (IDFA), but only if you allow tracking as described in section 3
  • Information about how you interact with ads, such as ads shown, tapped or watched
  • Crash and performance information of the ad service

This information is processed by Google under its own privacy policy and is not provided to us in a form that identifies you. Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

2.2 Apple (purchases, Game Center, diagnostics)

  • In-App Purchases: Premium (removes ads and unlocks all themes) and hint packs are sold through the App Store. Payments are processed entirely by Apple; we never receive your payment details. The App checks your purchases on your device to unlock what you bought and to restore purchases. Purchases made in earlier versions of Cyphre are recognized and unlock Premium.
  • Game Center: if you are signed in to Game Center on your device, the App reports the number of levels you have completed to a leaderboard and unlocks achievements. This is processed by Apple, uses your Game Center alias, and is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy and your Game Center privacy settings. You can turn Game Center off in iOS Settings.
  • App analytics and crash reports: if you chose to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous statistics and crash reports.

2.3 Nothing else

The App does not contain any other third-party advertising, analytics or social-media tools, and it does not use its own servers to collect information about you.

3. Ad Choices, Consent and Tracking

  • Consent form (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where required): when you first open the App, Google's consent form may ask you to accept, manage or decline the use of your information for ads. You can change your choice at any time in the App under Settings > Privacy & ad choices (shown in regions where it applies). If you decline, you may see fewer ads or ads that are not personalized.
  • App Tracking Transparency (iOS): the App asks whether it may track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads are still shown, but the advertising identifier is not used to personalize them. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Google ad settings: you can manage ad personalization at adssettings.google.com.
  • Remove ads: if you buy Premium, the App stops showing ads. On later launches it also does not start the advertising service, does not show the consent or tracking questions, and does not request ads.
  • US state privacy laws: we do not sell your personal information. Ad personalization by Google may count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under some state laws; you can opt out using the choices above.

4. How We Use Information

  • To run the game, save your progress and apply your settings
  • To unlock and restore what you purchased
  • To show, limit and measure ads (through Google AdMob)
  • To show your progress in Game Center leaderboards and achievements
  • To keep the App working and to fix problems

We do not use your information to build profiles about you or to make automated decisions that affect you.

5. Legal Bases and Your Rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, we rely on: performance of a contract (providing the game and your purchases); your consent (personalized ads and the use of the advertising identifier, which you can withdraw at any time); and our legitimate interest in keeping the App secure and working.

You have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or port personal data about you, to object to its processing, to withdraw consent, and to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority (in Romania: the National Supervisory Authority for Personal Data Processing, dataprotection.ro). Because we do not hold personal data about you, requests about data processed by Google or Apple are best made through their own privacy tools, and we will gladly help you do so. To contact us, see section 11.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Data stored on your device remains there until you reset your progress in the App or delete the App. Data processed by Google and Apple is kept according to their own policies. Because we do not collect your personal data on our own servers, there is nothing else for us to delete; you can, however, ask us about anything related to your privacy at any time.

7. International Transfers

Google and Apple may process information in countries outside your own, including the United States. Where required, they use safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or approved data protection frameworks.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. In some countries a higher age applies for giving consent to the processing of personal data (for example 16 in parts of the EEA); in those countries, please ask a parent or guardian to decide about the consent form. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can help.

9. Security

Your game data is stored in the App's protected storage on your device and is protected by the security features of iOS. We do not operate servers that hold your personal data. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App or the law changes. We will post the new version on this page and update the "Last updated" date above. Changes become effective when they are posted.
