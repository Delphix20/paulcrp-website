1. Scope and Who We Are

This Privacy Policy and Terms of Use (the "Policy") applies to the You Become iOS application (the "App"), its widgets, and related support communications. The App is developed and operated by the You Become development team ("we," "us," or "our"). For applicable privacy laws, the development team acts as the controller of personal data that it determines how and why to process.

You Become provides daily self-growth thoughts, selectable focus areas, favorites, progress features, reminders, widgets, sharing tools, and optional Premium subscriptions.

Local-first by design. The App does not require an account. Personal preferences and activity are kept on your device and are not uploaded to our content database.

Wellness notice. You Become offers general inspirational and educational self-development content. It is not medical, psychological, psychiatric, therapeutic, or other professional advice; it does not diagnose or treat any condition; and it is not an emergency or crisis service. Seek a qualified professional or local emergency service when appropriate.

2. Information the App Handles

Information stored locally

To provide and personalize the experience, the App may store the following information in its private app storage, iOS preferences, and the shared App Group used by You Become widgets:

  • Selected focus areas and content preferences.
  • Favorite thoughts and identifiers for thoughts you have viewed.
  • Last-seen dates, activity days, streak and milestone information, and other journey progress.
  • Onboarding status, selected app icon, haptic preferences, reminder settings, widget options, and interface state.
  • Daily free-use allowance, paywall display state, review-prompt eligibility, and local feature-event counters.
  • A locally cached indication of Premium entitlement and its expiration, obtained from verified App Store transactions.

This information is not used to create a server-side user profile and is not sent to our remote content database.

Content synchronization

The App periodically contacts our cloud-hosted content database to check for new or updated focus areas and thoughts. It downloads the editorial content and stores a copy on your device so the App can continue working offline. These requests do not include your favorites, selected focus areas, journey progress, or other personal settings.

As with most internet services, our database and infrastructure providers may process ordinary technical request data such as an IP address, timestamp, network or device headers, request path, and security logs to deliver, secure, and maintain the service. We do not use this information for advertising or to build behavioral profiles.

Notifications

If you opt in, the App uses Apple's local notification system to schedule daily thoughts and, if separately enabled, a reminder before a free trial ends. Notification choices, times, and thought selection are handled on your device. We do not use a remote push-notification profile or upload your notification schedule. You can change or revoke notification permission at any time in iOS Settings.

Widgets

If you add a You Become widget, the App shares selected content and widget preferences through its private App Group so WidgetKit can display them. Widget content may be visible on your Home Screen or Lock Screen, and iOS may retain widget snapshots as part of normal system operation. You control where widgets appear and can remove them at any time.

Sharing

When you share a thought, the App creates an image locally and presents Apple's system share sheet. The temporary image is removed after sharing completes when the system callback is available, or may later be cleared by iOS. Once you choose a destination, that app or service handles the shared content under its own privacy practices. We do not receive the recipient or destination you select.

Diagnostics and local analytics

The App does not include a third-party advertising or behavioral analytics SDK. It may write limited feature and monetization events to Apple's unified device log and keep aggregate counters locally to operate app flows. If you choose to share diagnostics with Apple, Apple may provide developers with crash, performance, and diagnostic information under your Apple privacy and analytics settings.

Support communications

If you email us, we receive the email address, message, and any information you voluntarily include. We use it to respond, provide support, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations. Please do not send health information or other sensitive personal information.

No storage or transmission method is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, uninterrupted availability, or that data retained by Apple, device backups, a sharing destination, or a service provider will never be accessed improperly.

7. Retention and Deletion

  • On-device data: retained until you remove individual items, change settings, reset relevant app data where available, or delete the App. Apple-managed backups or system snapshots may persist according to your Apple settings.
  • Cached editorial content: retained locally for offline use and replaced when a newer content release is downloaded.
  • Temporary shared images: retained only as needed for the sharing operation and then removed by the App or normal iOS cleanup.
  • Support email: retained only as long as reasonably needed to answer your request, maintain support records, prevent abuse, or satisfy legal obligations.
  • Provider records: Apple and our database or hosting providers retain transaction, technical, security, or diagnostic records according to their own policies and legal requirements.

Because there is no You Become account and we do not upload your personal preferences or activity, we normally cannot view or remotely delete data stored only on your device. Deleting the App removes its local container, subject to Apple backups and system behavior. Remove widgets separately if necessary, and cancel any subscription separately through Apple.
