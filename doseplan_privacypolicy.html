This page explains what DosePlan stores, how it uses that information, how subscriptions work, and the terms that apply when you use the app. DosePlan helps you organize a GLP-1 or other medication routine that a clinician has already prescribed: dose reminders and logs, refill reminders, symptom check-ins, daily routine targets, weight and progress photos, widgets, and a PDF report for your appointments. By using DosePlan, you agree to the practices and terms described here.

Before you start

Medical safety notice. DosePlan does not prescribe or recommend medication, does not calculate or recommend doses, dose changes, concentrations or injection volumes, does not diagnose symptoms, and does not tell you when to start, stop, increase, decrease, skip or combine any medication. Always follow your prescription label and the instructions from your doctor, pharmacist or other licensed clinician. Contact a clinician for medical questions and seek emergency help for urgent symptoms.
What DosePlan is. A personal tracking and organization tool. It is not a medical device, clinic, pharmacy, telehealth or emergency service, diagnostic tool, or a substitute for a licensed clinician, your medication label, medication guide or official care plan.
Local by design. DosePlan keeps your data on your device. It does not sync your data to iCloud or to any server of ours, and we cannot see, access or recover it.

2What DosePlan stores

Information you enter or create

DosePlan stores only what you choose to enter, import or create in the app:

  • Treatments: medication name (from a list of GLP-1 medications, or a name you type), form (pen, pill or vial), the dose from your prescription, how often you take it, dose time, start date, next dose and reminder settings.
  • Dose logs: date and time, medication, dose, form, injection site when applicable, whether the dose was late, and optional notes.
  • Supply: how many pens, pills or vials you have left, refill timing and refill reminders.
  • Daily routine: protein, water, fiber and movement targets and logs, and the support routines you pin.
  • Symptom check-ins: symptoms, severity, "no symptoms" entries, dates and notes.
  • Weight entries, your goal weight and weight entries imported from Apple Health.
  • Progress photos with their dates, tags, optional notes and the weight at the time, when available.
  • Notes you save for your doctor.
  • Settings: units, appearance, app language, notification choices, onboarding answers, and the data that widgets display.
  • Doctor report PDFs while you create, preview or share them.

Earlier versions of DosePlan included dose-step plans and vial reconstitution fields. These features have been removed, and DosePlan deletes that stored information from your device when you open version 1.2 or later.

Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, DosePlan asks for permission to read your step count and body weight. Steps fill in your daily movement routine and weight entries appear in your weight trend. DosePlan does not write any data to Apple Health. Apple Health data is used only to show you features inside the app, never for advertising, marketing or data mining, and it is never sold or shared with third parties. You can change this permission at any time in the Health app or in iOS Settings.

Camera and photos

When you add a progress photo, DosePlan uses the camera or the photo picker only after you start that action and allow access. Photos are saved inside the app on your device so you can compare your own progress. We never receive, review or use them.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, your device schedules these local reminders from the information you enter:

  • Dose reminders before or at your scheduled dose time, and a reminder if a dose has not been logged yet.
  • Refill reminders when your supply is running low.
  • If you start a free trial, a reminder two days before the trial ends, while it is set to renew.

Reminders can be delayed, silenced or missed because of Focus modes, device settings, battery state or iOS behavior. They are a convenience only; you remain responsible for following your medication label and your clinician's instructions.

Subscription status

DosePlan uses Apple's StoreKit to find out whether you have an active subscription or free trial and when a trial ends, so it can unlock the app and show your plan in Settings. It also remembers on your device whether the one-time yearly offer has already been shown. We do not receive your name, Apple ID or payment details.

No analytics, advertising or tracking

DosePlan contains no analytics, advertising or tracking tools, no third-party SDKs that collect data, and no account system. The app does not track you across apps or websites. Apple may give developers aggregated, anonymous App Store statistics and crash reports from people who chose to share them with app developers in iOS Settings; these do not include your DosePlan entries.

3Where your data is kept

On your device

Everything you enter in DosePlan is stored on your device, inside the app's private storage. DosePlan does not sync your data to iCloud or to any server of ours. If you delete the app, its data is deleted from that device.

Device backups

If you back up your device with iCloud Backup or to a computer, iOS may include DosePlan's data in that backup, and restoring the backup can bring it back. Backups are created, encrypted and stored by Apple or by your computer, under your control and Apple's terms.

Widgets

DosePlan's widgets read a small summary, such as your next dose, routine progress and supply, that the app saves in a shared space on your device. It never leaves your device and may be briefly out of date until iOS refreshes the widget.

Reports and shared files

Doctor reports are created as PDFs on your device for the period and sections you choose. If you save, print, send or share a report, that copy is handled by the app, person or service you choose, and we cannot control what happens to it afterwards. Treat reports, screenshots and shared files as sensitive health information.

4How DosePlan uses information

DosePlan uses the information on your device to:

  • Show your next dose, your last dose, overdue doses and your dose history.
  • Keep track of one or more treatments and their supply, and estimate when you may need a refill based on your entries.
  • Show routine progress, symptom patterns, weight trends, progress photos and insights.
  • Create the doctor report PDF you ask for.
  • Schedule the local reminders you allow.
  • Show widgets and remember your settings and language.
  • Check, unlock and restore your subscription through Apple.
  • Ask Apple to show its standard rating prompt from time to time; Apple decides whether it appears.

If you email us, we use what you send only to answer you.

  • Confirm that a medication, dose, schedule or route of administration is safe or right for you.
  • Contact a clinician, pharmacy, emergency service or caregiver for you.
  • Send your data to us or to anyone else unless you share it yourself.
  • Sell your data, or use Apple Health data for advertising or marketing.
  • Act as an electronic health record, pharmacy system, clinical decision support tool or provider portal.

6Medical and medication terms

Already prescribed treatment only

DosePlan is meant only for organizing treatment information you already have from a licensed clinician, your prescription or pharmacy label, or your medication guide. Do not use DosePlan to decide whether to start, change or stop any medication.

Your entries

Every medication name, dose, schedule and supply value in DosePlan is entered by you. DosePlan records and displays these values and counts your logged doses, but it does not verify that they are correct, prescribed or appropriate for you.

Symptoms

Symptom check-ins and patterns are personal notes only. DosePlan does not decide whether a symptom is normal, serious or related to a medication. If symptoms are severe, getting worse, unusual or worrying, contact a clinician or emergency services.

Weight, routine, nutrition and movement

Protein, water, fiber, movement, weight and photo features are personal tracking features. They are not a nutrition plan, exercise prescription or treatment plan and do not promise any result. The medical sources in the app are general references, not personal advice.

Reports for appointments

A doctor report summarizes what you have entered in DosePlan, so it can be incomplete or out of date. Review it before sharing. It is not a medical record unless your clinician chooses to treat it as one.

8Subscriptions, trials and payments

DosePlan Pro

DosePlan's tracking features require an active DosePlan Pro subscription or free trial. DosePlan Pro is offered as monthly and yearly auto-renewable subscriptions. Prices, currencies, taxes and trial availability are shown on the App Store purchase sheet and can vary by country and Apple account.

Free trial

Eligible new subscribers can start a 7-day free trial of the yearly plan. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, it automatically becomes a paid subscription and you are charged the price shown when you started. Apple decides trial eligibility; a trial is generally available once per subscriber.

One-time yearly offer

If you close the App Store purchase sheet for the yearly plan without buying, DosePlan may show a one-time offer for the yearly plan at a reduced price, including the free trial if you are eligible. It is shown once per device and renews at the price shown in the offer.

Renewal, cancellation and refunds

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple account settings, or from Settings in DosePlan. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Billing, refunds and cancellation timing are handled by Apple.

Restore purchases

The Restore option asks Apple for your current subscription status, for example after you reinstall DosePlan or move to a new device signed in with the same Apple account.

  • Camera and photos: change access in iOS Settings. Photos already saved in DosePlan stay until you delete them.

Because your data stays on your device and we do not receive it, we cannot access, export, correct or delete it for you. You control it directly in the app and on your device.

11Security and accuracy

DosePlan relies on iOS protections such as app sandboxing and data protection for files on your device. No system is perfectly secure, so protect your device with a passcode, keep your Apple account secure, and take care with backups, exported reports and shared files.

DosePlan is only as accurate as the information you enter or import. Medication names, doses, schedules, supply counts, weights, symptoms and reports can be wrong if the entries are wrong, incomplete or out of date. Check important information against your prescription and records.
